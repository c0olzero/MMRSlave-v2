MMRSlave - Dota 2 replay coach
==============================

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere you keep programs (for example Documents\MMRSlave).
   Keep the files together: MMRSlave.exe needs the _internal folder next to it.
2. Double-click MMRSlave.exe. It opens in its own window.
   Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
3. Click "Sign in through Steam". You sign in on Steam's own page; MMRSlave only learns your
   Steam ID, never your password.
4. In Dota 2, turn on Settings -> Social -> Expose Public Match Data, or your games can't be found.
5. Click "Fetch my latest games" and open one to review it.

Updates
-------
Settings (the gear at the bottom left) -> Check for updates. Tick "Install updates automatically
when MMRSlave starts" to stay up to date without thinking about it. Updates download only what
changed. When an update needs a new full download, the app links you to it; unzip it over this
folder and your games and settings stay.

Where your data lives
---------------------
%LOCALAPPDATA%\MMRSlave  (settings, downloaded games, logs, updates)
Delete that folder to start fresh.

Written coaching
----------------
The written report needs a Claude API key. Set the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY environment variable
(Windows: search "environment variables" -> Edit environment variables for your account),
then reopen MMRSlave. Everything else works without it.
